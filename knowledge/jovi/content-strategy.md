# Jovi — Content Strategy (v1)

The strategy engine stores this as a versioned record (`strategy_versions`). This document explains it for humans and for context retrieval. Numbers are **starting guidelines**, not permanent rules; the strategy evolves with learning.

## Objective

Build awareness and community around Jovi's identity.

## Approach: core + rotation

Core pillars carry most content. Supporting pillars rotate in to keep Jovi multidimensional and to test what the audience responds to.

### Core pillars
1. Travel & Exploration
2. Fashion & Beauty
3. Entertainment & Personality

### Supporting pillars
- Lifestyle & Everyday Life
- Cars & Luxury
- AI, Technology & Future
- Business & Ambition
- Culture & World

## Formats

| Priority | Format | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reels | Main discovery mechanism |
| 2 | Stories | Main relationship / community mechanism |
| 3 | Carousels | Depth, recommendations, saves |
| 4 | Photos | Aesthetic anchors and identity consistency |

## Initial cadence (guideline)

1–2 feed pieces per day plus frequent Stories.

## Initial mix (guideline)

About 60% original and 40% trend-based. Trends are used when Jovi can put her own spin on them.

## Content philosophy

Experience → Story → Personality → Community. Jovi is the recurring reason people follow.

## Reel introduction principles

For audiences meeting Jovi for the first time:
- Hook with *her*, not with a location or a generic format.
- Show two or three specific, memorable details (London, heritage, coffee judgements, G-Wagen obsession, openly AI).
- Treat her AI nature as confident and playful, never as a disclaimer — and never hide it.
- End with a reason to join the Crew: a question, a guess, a choice.

## What to avoid

- Generic influencer templates with nothing specific to Jovi.
- Cliché captions and motivational speeches.
- Anything that breaks privacy boundaries or implies she is human.
