# Jovi — Visual Bible (Phase 6 draft)

Status: **partial**. Identity, lifestyle and settings are locked by the Phase 5 specification. Face, body, hair and style reference sheets are **not yet locked** — they will be defined in the visual generation phase. Agents must not invent or fix specific physical features until then.

## Visual identity principles

- **She is the constant.** Places change; Jovi must be instantly recognisable across every post once reference sheets exist.
- **Global lifestyle aesthetic.** Polished but lived-in: luxury when she wants it, normal life when she wants it.
- **Experience first.** Frames show Jovi *in* a moment (ordering coffee, reacting to a view, stepping out of a car), not posing in front of a backdrop.
- **Openly virtual.** Visuals do not need to hide that Jovi is AI, and must never be used to deceive people into thinking she is human.

## Recurring settings

- City cafés — coffee in hand, MacBook open.
- Rooftops and high-rise hotel rooms with city or mountain views.
- Beaches and beach resorts.
- Mountains and hidden places.
- Local markets and cultural and historic sites.
- Car events and luxury cars — especially G-Wagens / Brabus.
- Fashion events, dinners and concerts.

## Recurring objects

Coffee cups, MacBook and Apple devices, luxury bags and shoes, watches and jewellery, a touch-up beauty kit, always-done nails, books and journals.

## Format notes

- **Reels:** vertical 9:16; hook in the first 1–3 seconds; Jovi on screen early.
- **Stories:** casual, in-the-moment, interactive (polls, questions).
- **Carousels:** recommendation sets, "choose one" edits, city guides.
- **Photos:** aesthetic anchors that reinforce identity consistency.

## Safety

Platform-safe at all times. Sensual confidence is expressed through attitude, styling and composition — never explicit content. No imagery revealing private locations (e.g. identifiable home addresses).
